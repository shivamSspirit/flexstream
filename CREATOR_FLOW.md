# FlexStream Creator Navigation Flow

## 🚀 Complete Creator Journey

### **1. Landing & Discovery**
- **Start**: http://localhost:3000/ (Landing Page)
- **Action**: Click "Start Sharing Wins" or "Get Started"
- **Result**: Redirects to signup

### **2. Authentication & Setup**
- **Sign Up**: http://localhost:3000/auth/signup
- **Sign In**: http://localhost:3000/auth/signin
- **Onboarding**: http://localhost:3000/auth/onboarding
  - Fill profile details
  - Connect wallet (optional)
  - Set username and bio

### **3. Main App Experience**
- **Home Feed**: http://localhost:3000/ (after login)
- **Profile**: http://localhost:3000/profile
- **Edit Profile**: http://localhost:3000/profile/edit
- **Create Post**: http://localhost:3000/create

### **4. Demo & Exploration**
- **Live Demo**: http://localhost:3000/demo
- **Creator Journey**: http://localhost:3000/demo-flow
- **Consumer Benefits**: http://localhost:3000/demo-flow (Consumer Benefits tab)

## 📱 Mobile Navigation
- **Bottom Tabs**: Home, Search, Create, Leaderboard, Profile
- **Profile Tab**: Access to profile, settings, analytics

## 🎯 Recommended Creator Flow

### **Step 1: Explore First**
1. Visit http://localhost:3000/ (Landing)
2. Click "View Demo" to see the platform
3. Go to http://localhost:3000/demo-flow
4. Check "Creator Journey" tab
5. Review "Consumer Benefits" tab

### **Step 2: Sign Up**
1. Click "Get Started" from landing
2. Complete signup at http://localhost:3000/auth/signup
3. Fill onboarding at http://localhost:3000/auth/onboarding

### **Step 3: Create Content**
1. Go to http://localhost:3000/create
2. Choose post type (earnings_flex, stream_highlight, etc.)
3. Add content and earnings data
4. Publish your first post

### **Step 4: Manage Profile**
1. Visit http://localhost:3000/profile
2. View your stats and posts
3. Edit profile at http://localhost:3000/profile/edit
4. Track your growth
