## HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

### **Tools:**
1. **Mailchimp** (free for <500 contacts)
2. **SendGrid** (free for 100 emails/day)
3. **Loops.so** (built for startups)
4. **ConvertKit** (creator-focused)

### **Process:**
1. Run `node scripts/export-waitlist.js`
2. Upload CSVs to email tool
3. Replace `[REFERRAL_CODE]` with merge tag
4. Schedule emails:
   - Day 1: Top 50
   - Day 2: Next 50
   - Day 3: Remaining
5. Track opens/clicks
6. Follow up with non-openers

### **Personalization:**
- Use first name if you have it
- Segment by referral count (reward top referrers)
- A/B test subject lines
- Send from personal email (higher open rate)

### **Best Practices:**
- Send at 9 AM or 6 PM (highest open rates)
- Mobile-optimize (70% read on phone)
- Keep it short (under 200 words)
- Clear CTA (one click to access)
- Personal tone (like a DM, not marketing)
