## Executive Summary

The prediction market space on Solana has exploded, with weekly volumes exceeding $2B. Key trends:
1. **Creator Economy Integration** - Melee, Worm leading with creator monetization
2. **Social Virality** - One-click sharing, Twitter/X integration crucial
3. **Permissionless Markets** - Users create markets, not just bet
4. **Gamification** - Points, streaks, leaderboards drive retention
5. **Low Friction** - Solana's speed/cost enables micro-betting

## Key Patterns for PMF

### What's Working:

1. **Creator Monetization**
   - Melee: 20% fees to creators
   - Worm: Creator fees
   - DuelDuck: Mentionboard rewards

2. **Social Virality**
   - One-click share to X/Discord
   - Creator attribution visible
   - Meme-friendly market titles

3. **Gamification**
   - Leaderboards (all platforms)
   - Streaks (Trepa)
   - Points systems (DuelDuck)
   - Badges/achievements

4. **Low Friction Entry**
   - Social login (Telegram, Gmail)
   - Wallet-first but not wallet-only
   - Mobile-optimized

5. **Permissionless Creation**
   - Anyone creates markets
   - No central approval
   - Cultural/meme topics allowed

### What's NOT Working:

1. **Pure Binary Markets** (without social layer)
2. **Complex UI** (trading terminal style for retail)
3. **Wallet-Only Access** (limits reach)
4. **Politics/Finance Only** (misses Gen Z)
5. **No Creator Incentives** (no viral loop)

## UI/UX Best Practices

### Color Palettes That Work:
- **Dark Mode Dominant**: #070707, #111, #171717
- **Accent Colors**: Neon green (#E0FF62, #bce67a), Yellow (#ffc54d)
- **High Contrast**: White text on dark

### Typography:
- Clean sans-serif (Inter, system fonts)
- Large probability numbers
- Minimal text, maximum clarity

### Card Design:
- Clear question/title
- Prominent probability %
- Yes/No buttons always visible
- Volume/liquidity stats
- Time remaining

### Mobile First:
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Swipeable cards
- Bottom navigation
- One-thumb operation

### Viral Features:
- Share button prominent
- Creator attribution
- Leaderboard teaser
- Social proof (X active, Y betting)
